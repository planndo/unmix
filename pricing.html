      Billing questions

      Can I cancel anytime?

      Yes. Open your account page, choose Manage billing and cancel. You keep Pro until the end of the period you've paid for, and then your account moves to Free.

      What happens to my songs if I cancel?

      Unmix doesn't store your songs, so there is nothing to lose. Any files you exported are on your device and remain yours.

      Can I switch between monthly and yearly?

      Yes, from Manage billing on your account page.

      When does the free allowance reset?

      On the first day of each month (UTC). Unused songs don't carry over.

      Does Pro make separation faster?

      No. Separation runs on your own device on every plan, so its speed depends on your hardware.
