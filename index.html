Questions

Is my music really not uploaded?

Yes. The separation runs in your browser tab using models that are downloaded to your device. Your audio stays there. Signing in only sends your account details and a count of songs used.

What do I need to run it?

A recent version of Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari. A computer with a suitable GPU is fastest, and any other device falls back to the CPU, which takes longer. The models are a one-time download of up to about 140 MB depending on what you use.

How good is the separation?

Very good on most modern recordings, but no AI separation is perfect. You may hear faint traces of one instrument in another stem, especially on dense or older mixes.

Can I use it on any song?

You are responsible for having the rights to whatever you process, and to whatever you do with the results. Practising along at home and publishing a remix are very different things, so check first.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel from your account page in a couple of clicks. You keep Pro until the end of the period you've paid for, and nothing further is charged.
