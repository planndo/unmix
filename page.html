Questions

Good to know

How do I remove vocals from a song?

Open Remove vocals, drop in an MP3, WAV, FLAC, M4A or OGG file and wait while the AI separates it. You get an instrumental (karaoke) track and an a cappella vocal track. Use the faders to keep a little of the voice, then download as WAV or MP3.

Is Timbila really a free vocal remover?

Yes. Removing vocals, the mixer, and WAV and MP3 downloads are free with no account and no watermark. Plus and Pro add 4-stem separation, noise removal, more formats and longer files for a single payment.

Can it split drums, bass and other instruments?

Split stems (Plus) separates a song into vocals, drums, bass and other. Timbila also recognises the instruments in the mix, such as piano, guitar, saxophone, strings or marimba, and gives each one a Focus EQ so you can turn it up or down.

How does the noise removal work?

Clean audio (Pro) detects and notches out 50 or 60 Hz mains hum, learns the steady background noise of your recording to remove hiss and room noise, and uses the RNNoise neural network to take traffic, fans and chatter out from behind a voice. You can compare before and after while you adjust it.

Which formats can I convert between?

Timbila reads almost any audio or video file, including MP3, WAV, FLAC, M4A, AAC, OGG, Opus, WMA, AIFF, MP4, MOV and WebM. It writes WAV and MP3 for free, and FLAC, M4A (AAC), OGG Vorbis and AIFF with Plus. Extracting the audio from a video is the same one step.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. All processing happens inside your browser. The AI models download once (about 30 MB for the vocal remover) and are cached, so later songs start straight away and work offline.

Why is it faster on some computers?

Timbila uses your graphics card through WebGPU when the browser supports it (Chrome and Edge on most computers), and several CPU threads otherwise. The first run also downloads the AI model once; after that, songs start straight away.

What does "pay once" mean?

Plus and Pro are one-time purchases with a license key, not subscriptions. Enter the key once on each device you use.
