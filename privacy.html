Your audio

Songs you open in Unmix are processed in your browser on your own device. They are not uploaded to us or to anyone else, and we can't access them. We don't record song titles or file names.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, a securely hashed password or your Google sign-in identity, and when you accepted the terms.
  • Usage count: how many songs you've started each month, to apply the plan allowance.
  • Subscription details: your Stripe customer reference, plan, status and renewal date. Card details go directly to Stripe and never reach us.

Why we use it

To run your account and provide what you've signed up for (performing our contract with you), to take payment, to meet legal and tax duties, and to protect the service from abuse (our legitimate interests). We don't sell your data and we don't use advertising or analytics trackers.

Who processes it for us

  • Supabase: accounts and database.
  • Stripe: payments and invoices, as an independent controller for some purposes.
  • Google: sign-in if you choose it, and web fonts, which means your IP address reaches Google when a page loads.
  • GitHub Pages: website hosting, including standard server logs.

Some of these providers process data outside your country, under their own safeguards. [Confirm your Supabase region and transfer safeguards.]

What we store in your browser

We use local storage to keep you signed in and to count the free song you can use without an account, and the browser's cache to keep the AI models so they only download once. These are needed for the service to work, so there is no cookie banner.

How long we keep it

We keep your data until you delete your account, which removes your account, usage and subscription records from our database straight away. Stripe keeps invoice records as tax law requires.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask to access, correct, export or delete your data, or object to how it's used, and you can complain to your data protection authority. You can delete your account yourself on the account page, or contact us for anything else.

Changes

If we change this policy materially, we will tell you before the changes apply.
